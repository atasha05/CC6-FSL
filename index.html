STEP 1

Create a word label

STEP 2

Record examples

Select a label, perform the entire sign after the countdown, and keep your hands visible until recording finishes. Each example captures 30 consecutive frames.

Camera must be ready before recording.

For a useful classroom test, collect 15–30 complete sequences per word and at least two labels. Vary your speed and position slightly. Include a “NONE” label with unrelated movements if you want a rejection category.

STEP 3

Train your model

The browser trains a neural-network classifier on ordered sequences of hand landmarks, so the timing and movement across 30 frames can help distinguish signs.

Add at least two labels and record examples for each.

Camera frames and training examples are processed in this browser and saved locally on this device. They are not uploaded by this demo. Use only with permission if recording other people.
